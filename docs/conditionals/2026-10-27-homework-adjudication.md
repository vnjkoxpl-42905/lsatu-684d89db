## Accepted items and explicit repairs

In the derivations below, `NOT` applies to the stated complete idea, `AND` requires both, and `OR` permits one or both. Each pair of rules is equivalent, not two independent claims.

### hw-g3-badge-20261027

- Source: `08 Group 3 (Sep 10)/Homework/2025-09-10_Group3Conditionals.pdf`, page 3, Section 2 item 1. Key: sibling `Answer Key/2025-09-10_Group 3 Answer Key.pdf`, page 3, inspected as text and rendered pixels.
- Original: “Unless the badge is scanned, entry will not open at the turnstile.”
- Original key: NOT badge scanned → entry not open; its contrapositive negates the complete idea “entry not open.” Correct, including the graphical stroke lost in text extraction.
- Authored meaning: entry opens at this turnstile → badge scanned. Equivalent: NOT badge scanned → NOT entry opens. Both labels are positive predicates; the target retains the original negative correctly.
- Boundary: scanned and closed is allowed. Unscanned and open is forbidden. Scanned → open is rejected.
- Destination: Group 3 independent practice; simple existing-negative task.

### hw-g3-carbon-20261027

- Source: `06 Group 3 and 4 Homework PT 1/Homework/2025-07-03_Group3&4 homework PT 1 (1).pdf`, page 1, Question 1. Key: sibling `Answer Key/2025-07-03_Answer key for group 3 and 4 HW  (1).pdf`, page 1, rendered and read.
- Original: “Plant life cannot survive without atmospheric carbon.”
- Original key: PL → AC and NOT AC → NOT PL. Correct underlying relationship; “without atmospheric carbon” is incorrectly listed as an idea in its procedural wording.
- Authored meaning: plant life can survive → atmospheric carbon present. Equivalent: atmospheric carbon absent → plant life cannot survive. Ability to survive is retained rather than silently changed to actual survival. “Without” is excluded from the idea definition.
- Boundary: carbon present while survival is impossible is allowed. Survival possible without carbon is forbidden. Carbon → ability to survive is rejected.
- Destination: Group 3 independent practice. The joint filename remains unchanged, but this item is Group 3 rather than a mixed-family exercise.

### hw-g3-paragraph-20261027

- Source: September Group 3 homework, page 10, Section 3 item 3, “Lecture Hall.” Key: page 13, rendered and read.
- Original rule within the paragraph: “Unless the presenter arrives by ten after the hour, the teaching assistant begins the lecture and records attendance.”
- Original key: NOT arrival by deadline → begins lecture AND records attendance. Contrapositive English correctly uses NOT begins OR NOT records. Correct underlying meaning. The rendered combined negation also confirms that the text-only diagram was incomplete evidence.
- Adaptation: retains the original rule and deadline, reduces unrelated context to announcements and laptop permission, and asks the learner to find the unless sentence. The builder grades the conditional construction, not an independently selected text span.
- Authored meaning: NOT arrival by deadline → begins AND records. Equivalent: NOT begins OR NOT records → arrival by deadline.
- Boundary: a missed deadline with only one promised action is forbidden. Timely arrival without either action remains allowed. Beginning and recording together do not prove a missed deadline.
- Destination: later Group 3 independent paragraph practice, after simple items and prior AND/OR instruction.

### hw-mixed-career-or-20261027

- Source: July AND/OR homework, page 1 item 2. Original: “You won't be successful in your career or personal life without good time management.” Key: page 1, rendered and read.
