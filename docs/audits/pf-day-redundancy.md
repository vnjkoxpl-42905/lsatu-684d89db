# PF Day-by-Day Redundancy Audit

**Source:** `src/bootcamps/principle-flaw/data.ts:1014` (`scheduleData`)
**Method:** for each day, summarize `(focus, open, read, do, review, deliverable, intensity, time)`. Then flag days whose *template shape* (kinds of items in each bucket, not the specific resource IDs) repeats a prior day.

**Note:** flags below are **suggestions**, not deletions. The user reviews and decides whether to consolidate.

---

## Day 1: Flaw Foundation · Monday
- **Focus:** Flaw Foundation
- **Plain goal:** Learn the basic Flaw method and start Level 1.
- **Intensity / Time:** Medium · 75–100 min
- **Open:** `flaw-packet`
- **Read:** `flaw-notes-strategy`
- **Do:** `flaw-worksheet`, `flaw-pm-1`
- **Review topics:** Conclusion · Evidence · Assumption · Flaw · Tempting wrong answer
- **Bring:** 2 confusing questions for tutoring
- **Deliverable:** Worksheet completed and Level 1 misses logged
- **Redundancy flag:** none — kickoff day, unique shape (worksheet + first PM set).

## Day 2: Tutoring Prep & Light Review · Tuesday
- **Focus:** Tutoring Prep & Light Review
- **Plain goal:** Review Monday's work and prepare questions for tutoring.
- **Intensity / Time:** Light · 30–45 min
- **Open:** `flaw-packet`
- **Read:** Monday's Review Log entries
- **Do:** Redo 2 hardest questions from Monday
- **Review topics:** Process efficiency · Clarity of explanations · Specific confusion points
- **Bring:** List of 2-3 specific questions for tutoring
- **Deliverable:** Ready for tutoring session
- **Redundancy flag:** **Template = "Tutoring Prep"** — same structural shape recurs on Day 9 (`Tuesday W2`). Both: Light/Medium intensity, prep-for-session, redo + review log + bring list. *Same template, different content focus (Day 9 layers in Conform). Probably intentional weekly cadence; not redundant.*

## Day 3: Principle Foundation · Wednesday
- **Focus:** Principle Foundation
- **Plain goal:** Learn the Principle framework and start basic drills.
- **Intensity / Time:** High · 60–90 min
- **Open:** `principle-packet`, `flaw-packet`
- **Read:** `principle-recap`
- **Do:** `principle-strengthen-hw`, Redo 2 Flaw Level 1 questions
- **Review topics:** Rule identification · Trigger mapping · Missing link · Matching outcome
- **Bring:** 1 Principle question that felt slow
- **Deliverable:** Principle mapping completed and Flaw re-dos logged
- **Redundancy flag:** none — first Principle-Strengthen day, unique shape.

## Day 4: Flaw Level 2 & Precision · Thursday
- **Focus:** Flaw Level 2 & Precision
- **Plain goal:** Master harder flaws and refine answer choice breakdown.
- **Intensity / Time:** Medium · 75–100 min
- **Open:** `flaw-packet`
- **Read:** `flaw-notes-advanced`
- **Do:** `flaw-pm-2`
- **Review topics:** Label every answer choice · Identify the 'too strong' trap · Spot 'bad match' errors
- **Bring:** A 'too strong' wrong answer you liked
- **Deliverable:** Level 2 completed with full answer breakdowns
- **Redundancy flag:** **Template = "Flaw Level N step-up"** — recurs on Day 8 (`Level 3`) and Day 13 (`Level 4`). All three are: Medium intensity, one packet open, one strategy read, one pm-N set, level-themed review. *Three days share the template with only the level integer changing.* Worth confirming this is the intended cadence.

## Day 5: Principle Application · Friday
- **Focus:** Principle Application
- **Plain goal:** Apply principles to strengthen arguments and check maintenance.
- **Intensity / Time:** Medium · 60–90 min
- **Open:** `principle-packet`, `flaw-packet`
- **Read:** `principle-app-strategy`
- **Do:** `principle-strengthen-hw`, `strengthen-app-hw`, Redo 2 Flaw misses
- **Review topics:** Identify the proven condition · Check for necessary vs sufficient · Verify the result
- **Bring:** 1 Application question with a tricky 'if'
- **Deliverable:** Principle and Application homework completed
- **Redundancy flag:** **`do[0]` re-runs `principle-strengthen-hw` from Day 3.** Day 3's `do` already included this set. If a student did Day 3 in earnest, repeating the same PracticeSet on Day 5 isn't new exposure. *Consider: drop `principle-strengthen-hw` from Day 5's `do`, OR Day 5 should pull a different Strengthen set.*

## Day 6: Timed Practice · Saturday

- **Bring:** Your worst miss from the timed section
- **Deliverable:** Section score and comprehensive review log
- **Redundancy flag:** **Template = "Full timed LR + comprehensive review"** — recurs on Day 12 (`Final Timed LR`). Both have identical shape: High intensity · 120 min · `Schedule` open · `timed-strategy-lr` read · one timed section · post-mortem review. *Two days share this exact template; this is presumably the weekly checkpoint cadence (week 1 baseline, week 2 retest). Not redundant — by design.*

## Concrete consolidation candidates (for your review)

1. **`principle-conform-hw` appears 3× (Days 9, 10, 11).** Split into `-1`, `-2`, `-3` so each day is a fresh set, or drop from Day 11.
2. **`flaw-pm-3` appears 3× (Days 8, 10, 13).** Day 13's focus says "Level 4" but the set is still `flaw-pm-3` — likely a missing `flaw-pm-4` set or a mis-labeled focus.
3. **`flaw-notes-strategy` read on both Day 1 and Day 8.** Confirm intentional reinforcement or replace Day 8 with `flaw-notes-advanced` (currently on Day 4).
4. **`strengthen-app-hw` appears on Day 5 and Day 8.** Same re-run issue.
5. **Day 10 ("Mixed Drills") doesn't introduce new question exposure** — it re-runs Day 8's Flaw set + Day 9's Conform set. If the goal is mixed *task identification*, that's defensible; if the goal is *new mixed practice*, swap one of the sets.
6. **Day 13 RC content is outside the PF / LR scope.** Confirm whether RC belongs in PF at all; if not, drop or move.

None of these are deletions — they're flags for your judgment.
