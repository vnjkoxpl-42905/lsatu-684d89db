# Conditional Logic Bootcamp

The shared home for conditional reasoning lessons, activities, practice and flashcards.

## Terms

**Conditional Logic Bootcamp**
The current course and central place to learn and practise conditional reasoning, including conditional activities previously presented elsewhere.

**Focused assignment**
An assignment of one named activity, with a topic or mode selected where appropriate, instead of the full bootcamp.

**Flashcards**
Cards for recalling and checking a conditional concept, translation or comparison, with filters for the relevant practice.

**Activity**
A named exercise collection, such as Conditional Rephrase or Missing Links. An activity can be opened inside the bootcamp or assigned on its own.

**Mode and topic filter**
Settings that choose the task or narrow the material within an activity. They do not create a second question collection.

**Guided set**
An existing LSAT question-bank activity with its own instructions, selected pool, attempts and saved history.
