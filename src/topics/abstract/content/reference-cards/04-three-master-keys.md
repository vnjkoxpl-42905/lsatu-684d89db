# The Three Master Keys

**Synopsis**
Three LSAT-ese terms that unlock more answer choices than any others. Memorize the translations.

| LSAT term | Translation |
|---|---|
| Explanation | Cause |
| Hypothesis | Potential cause (a guess at what caused something) |
| Presuppose | Require (a necessary condition) |

**How to use**
Every time you see one of these in an answer choice, mentally swap in the translation. Then ask whether the stimulus actually does that thing.
