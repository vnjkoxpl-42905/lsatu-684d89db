# FABS and the Indicator Buckets

**Synopsis**
Indicator words tell you what slot the next clause belongs to. Memorize the buckets.

**Support indicators (FABS)**
**F**or, **A**s, **B**ecause, **S**ince. Anything after these points backward to evidence.

**Conclusion indicators**
Therefore, thus, hence, so, it follows that, consequently, clearly, this shows that, then.

**Pivot indicators**
But, yet, however, on the other hand, nevertheless. Crossing from one viewpoint to another.

**Opposing-view indicators**
Most people think, some contend, it has been said, some critics have argued, it is widely believed.

**Concession indicators**
Granted, while, although, naturally, though it is true.

**Opinion indicators (word-level)**
Ought, should, must, need to, likely, probably, will.

**How to use**
Read the stimulus. Underline every indicator word. The skeleton lights up automatically.
