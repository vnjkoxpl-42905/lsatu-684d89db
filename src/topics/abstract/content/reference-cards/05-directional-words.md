# The Directional Word Cheat Sheet

**Synopsis**
X/Y phrases use directional words to tell you which side is the conclusion and which is the evidence.

**Backward-pointing (next is evidence)**
- on the basis of
- on the grounds that
- because
- since
- from
- by
- given that

**Forward-pointing (next is conclusion)**
- to support
- to establish
- to demonstrate
- in order to
- so that
- thereby

**How to use**
Find the directional word first. Whatever follows a backward-pointer is evidence. Whatever follows a forward-pointer is conclusion. Map X and Y accordingly.
