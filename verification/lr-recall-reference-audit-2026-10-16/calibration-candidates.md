## PT101.S2.Q1: argument / scope

App item: `PT101_S2_Q1`

Source proposition: Press release: A comprehensive review evaluating the medical studies done up to the present time has found no reason to think that drinking coffee in normal amounts harms the coffee-drinker's heart. So coffee drinkers can relax and enjoy their beverage—it is safe to drink coffee.

Written CLIR (Loophole): What if just because coffee isn't bad for your heart that doesn't mean it's safe overall? What if coffee makes your hair fall out?

Paraphrase locator: “we have no reason to think, based on these studies, that drinking coffee in normal amounts”

Analysis locator: “What if just because it's not bad for your heart doesn't mean it doesn't have other adverse effects?”

Grading focus: Preserve no reason to think, heart-specific finding, normal amounts and broader safety conclusion; speculation about harms is reaction, not a stimulus fact.

Review caution: The transcript sometimes upgrades no reason to think harm into not going to harm; this is not automatically faithful.

## PT106.S2.Q6: inference / conditional reasoning

App item: `PT106_S2_Q6`

Source proposition: The Rienzi, a passenger ship, sank as a result of a hole in its hull, possibly caused by sabotage. Normally, when a holed ship sinks as rapidly as the Rienzi did, water does not enter the ship quickly enough for the ship to be fully flooded when it reaches the ocean floor. Full flooding can be achieved, however, by sabotage. Any ship that sinks deep into the ocean when not fully flooded will implode. Deep-sea photographs, taken of the sunken Rienzi where it rests on the ocean floor, reveal that the Rienzi did not implode.

Written CLIR (Inference): If water did not enter the Rienzi abnormally quickly, it was sabotaged.

Paraphrase locator: “water can't get in quickly enough for it to be completely, fully 100% flooded by the time it reaches the ocean floor”

Analysis locator: “Unless it was, you know, there was something abnormal about the ship”

Grading focus: Preserve normally, possibly sabotaged, not-fully-flooded implies implosion, observed no implosion, conditional sabotage inference rather than definite sabotage.

Review caution: Transcript says explodes/blown up while correcting to imploded. Name variants and holed/hold are ASR risks. Do not adopt physical reversal as a faithful detail.
